Design Principles

Simple & Clear

No overwhelming data. Everything is visual, intuitive, and easy to understand at a glance.

AI is Working FOR You

Proactive AI insights and live activity feeds show the system is actively managing your business.

Conversational First

Chat-based onboarding and expansion flows feel natural, not like filling out forms.

Visual Status Indicators

Green dots, status badges, and live metrics make it obvious what's working and what needs attention.

Technology-Phobia Friendly

Plain language ("Clients" not "Contacts"), visual workflows, undo/edit anytime - no technical jargon.

Lego-Style Modular

Dynamic capability assembly - build only what you need, reuse what you have.
