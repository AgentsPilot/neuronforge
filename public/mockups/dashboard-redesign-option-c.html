Design Notes - Option C: Value Story

  • Focus: Narrative journey - "What you've accomplished" as the hero
  • Hero: Big impact number with goal progress - motivating and tangible
  • Structure: Three story cards - Working / Attention / Opportunity

  • Benefit: Users feel accomplished, not overwhelmed
