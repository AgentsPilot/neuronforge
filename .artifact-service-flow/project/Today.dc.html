Four claims about one unanswered question

Nobody has said what this costs. The screen says it four ways, and they disagree.

“No price set yet”

The note under the chips. Correct: the field is empty and the question is open.

“Payment Plan — Free”

Reads as a setting called Payment Plan whose value is Free. It is neither: it is the empty price, relabelled.

“Nothing is collected. This service is free.”

The summary panel, stating as fact the thing the note beside it calls unanswered.

“They pay by card” — selected

A collection method chosen for an amount that does not exist, on a service the panel has just called free.

The cause, in one line

Empty is read as unanswered in one place and as free in two others — and the chip row asks two different questions at once: how an amount arrives (card, invoice) and whether there is an amount at all (quote).

That is why neither order worked. Chips first broke the ones needing a figure; price first broke the quote. One row, two jobs.

The fix is the split, not the order. Ask what it costs — free, a set price, per job — and only the middle answer has anything left to ask.
