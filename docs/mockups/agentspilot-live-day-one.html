When the advisor switches on

Not all at once. The Insight card appears on day one — but at day one it has only one thing to say, and it's about setup. Each of the seven vectors lights independently as its own evidence arrives: wins need no history at all, conversion needs ~25 visitors, cash flow needs a first late invoice, retention needs clients old enough to lapse, pricing needs six weeks of calendar. The strip under the card shows which are reading and says plainly why the others aren't.

The moment that matters commercially is Day 90, not Day 1: the third recurrence is when the advisor stops asking and offers to take the job. Recommend-and-run works on a single instance; a standing rule needs a repeat, or the threshold is guessed from one data point.

Why the map isn't just "0 everywhere"

Three devices do the work. Blueprint stations — an unbuilt station is drawn as a dashed plan, not a zero, so an empty map reads as "not switched on yet" instead of "nothing is happening." The confidence bar — the Pilot refuses to call a leak until it has the numbers to call one, and says so out loud with a threshold. First light — three milestones the user waits for, so week one has something to anticipate instead of something to stare at.
